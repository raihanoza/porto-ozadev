# How to Create Your Open Graph (OG) Image

## What is an Open Graph Image?
An OG image is what appears when you share your website link on social media platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.

## Specifications
- **Dimensions**: 1200 x 630 pixels
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File Size**: Under 300KB (ideally under 100KB)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1.91:1
- **File Name**: `og-image.jpg` or `og-image.png`
- **Location**: `/public/image/og-image.jpg`

## Design Guidelines

### What to Include:
1. **Your Name/Brand** - Large, readable text
   - Example: "Raihan Oza Samudera Siregar"
   
2. **Your Title/Role**
   - Example: "Software Engineer & Full Stack Developer"
   
3. **Key Skills or Technologies** (optional)
   - Icons or text: React, Next.js, TypeScript
   
4. **Your Photo** (optional but recommended)
   - Professional headshot
   - Positioned on left or right side
   
5. **Background**
   - Clean, professional
   - Brand colors (use your portfolio theme colors)
   - Subtle gradients or patterns

### Design Tools:
1. **Canva** (Recommended for beginners)
   - Free templates available
   - Custom size: 1200 x 630 px
   - URL: https://www.canva.com

2. **Figma** (For designers)
   - Professional tool
   - Free tier available
   - URL: https://www.figma.com

3. **Adobe Photoshop**
   - Professional image editing
   - More control over design

4. **Online OG Image Generators**
   - https://www.opengraph.xyz/
   - https://og-playground.vercel.app/

## Step-by-Step with Canva

1. **Create Account**
   - Go to https://www.canva.com
   - Sign up for free

2. **Create Custom Size**
   - Click "Create a design"
   - Select "Custom size"
   - Enter: 1200 x 630 pixels

3. **Add Background**
   - Choose gradient or solid color
   - Use your brand colors

4. **Add Your Text**
   - Your name (large, bold)
   - Your title (medium size)
   - Skills/technologies (smaller)

5. **Add Your Photo** (optional)
   - Upload your professional photo
   - Crop to circular or square shape
   - Position on left or right

6. **Add Icons/Elements**
   - Search for tech icons (React, Next.js, etc.)
   - Add subtle decorative elements

7. **Download**
   - Click "Download"
   - Choose JPG (smaller file size)
   - Save as `og-image.jpg`

8. **Place in Project**
   - Move file to `/public/image/og-image.jpg`

## Testing Your OG Image

After creating and uploading:

1. **Facebook Sharing Debugger**
   - URL: https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/
   - Enter your website URL
   - Click "Scrape Again" to refresh

2. **Twitter Card Validator**
   - URL: https://cards-dev.twitter.com/validator
   - Enter your website URL
   - Preview how it looks

3. **LinkedIn Post Inspector**
   - URL: https://www.linkedin.com/post-inspector/
   - Enter your website URL
   - Check preview

## Pro Tips

1. **Text Readability**
   - Use high contrast (white text on dark background)
   - Font size minimum: 60-80px for main text
   - Avoid thin or decorative fonts

2. **Safe Area**
   - Keep important content 100px from edges
   - Some platforms crop images

3. **Mobile Preview**
   - Check how it looks on mobile
   - Text should still be readable when small

4. **File Optimization**
   - Use https://tinypng.com/ to compress
   - Reduce file size without losing quality

5. **Consistency**
   - Use same colors as your website
   - Match your overall brand style
